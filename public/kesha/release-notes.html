Release Notes

KESHA is in development. Here's where things stand.

Pre-launchOctober 9, 2026

The waitlist is open

Kesha is in development: an iPhone and CarPlay ride-along for drivers on long roads at night.

Here is what we are building first:

  • A voice-first drive flow with almost no taps while moving
  • Music and sound cues that change with alertness
  • Black-spot warnings before dangerous stretches
  • A CarPlay flame that acts like Now Playing artwork and a Live Activity
