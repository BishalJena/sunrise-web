Release Notes

BERSIH is in development. Here's where things stand.

Pre-launchOctober 9, 2026

The waitlist is open

BERSIH is in development: an iPhone share-sheet action that makes a clean copy before a photo or video leaves your phone.

Here is what we are building first:

  • Removal of location, date, time, phone and camera metadata
  • A clean-copy flow that keeps the original unchanged
  • Support for photos, multiple photos and video
  • Fully on-device processing
