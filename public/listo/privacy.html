Privacy Policy

LISTO is built around sensitive travel plans. This policy explains what this website collects today and how the app is designed to handle trip data.

This website and the waitlist

When you join the waitlist, we store your email address, which app you signed up for, and the time you signed up. We also store your approximate location (country, region, city and time zone, estimated from your IP address by Cloudflare; we don't store the IP address itself) and how you found this site (the page you signed up on, the site that linked you here, and any campaign tags in the link). We use this only to tell you about LISTO's launch and major updates, and to understand where interest in it comes from. We don't sell or share it, and every email includes a way to unsubscribe.

Waitlist sign-ups are stored with our hosting provider, Cloudflare. To remove your email from the waitlist, contact us at hello@lotus-f2.app.

This site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies or track you across other websites.

Trip details (once the app launches)

Trips and document checklists are designed to stay on your device. If you turn on iCloud, they sync through your private iCloud account.

Rule checks

We may use anonymous route demand to decide which passport and country rules to curate next. We do not sell travel plans.

Changes to this policy

LISTO hasn't launched yet. We'll update this policy before launch to reflect exactly how the app works, and note material changes in the release notes.

Questions? Reach us at hello@lotus-f2.app.
