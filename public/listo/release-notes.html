Release Notes

LISTO is in development. Here's where things stand.

Pre-launchOctober 9, 2026

The waitlist is open

LISTO is in development for travellers whose routes depend on the passport they hold, the countries they transit, and the rules at the counter.

Here is what we are building first:

  • A trip table with documents, dependencies and missing items
  • A true total that includes fees, insurance and visa wait time
  • Official-source handoffs with checked dates
  • An airport walk and a counter pass to show what is ready
