Privacy Policy

Underneath is built to inform you, not to track you. This policy explains the waitlist today and the app we are building.

This website and the waitlist

When you join the waitlist, we store your email address, which app you signed up for, and the time you signed up. We also store your approximate location (country, region, city and time zone, estimated from your IP address by Cloudflare; we don't store the IP address itself) and how you found this site (the page you signed up on, the site that linked you here, and any campaign tags in the link). We use this only to tell you about Underneath's launch and major updates, and to understand where interest in it comes from. We don't sell or share it, and every email includes a way to unsubscribe.

Waitlist sign-ups are stored with our hosting provider, Cloudflare. To remove your email from the waitlist, contact us at hello@lotus-e3.app.

This site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies or track you across other websites.

Location and interests (once the app launches)

Your chosen topics and approximate location are used to prepare an edition. You can use a city instead of precise location. We do not sell reading habits or share them with advertisers.

Changes to this policy

Underneath hasn't launched yet. We'll update this policy before launch to reflect exactly how the app works, and note material changes in the release notes.

Questions? Reach us at hello@lotus-e3.app.
