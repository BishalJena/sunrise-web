Release Notes

Underneath is in development. Here's where things stand.

Pre-launchOctober 9, 2026

The waitlist is open

Underneath is in development: a finishable news edition for people who get too much noise from feeds and chats.

Here is what we are building first:

  • One daily edition with a visible end
  • Rumour bills that peel away to reveal checked stories
  • Signals for confirmed, developing, unverified and false stories
  • Sources and proof built into sharing
