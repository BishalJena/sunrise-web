What HUSH can and cannot do

HUSH helps you check for devices that can see or hear you, but no tool can detect every device. A clear check is not a guarantee, and a flagged device is not proof of wrongdoing.

If you believe you are in danger, contact local emergency services. If you find a device, do not tamper with it. Report it to the property and the police.

Lawful use

Use HUSH only to check spaces you are staying in or are responsible for. Do not use it to interfere with other people's networks or devices.
