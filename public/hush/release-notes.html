Release Notes

HUSH is in development. Here's where things stand.

Pre-launchOctober 9, 2026

The waitlist is open

HUSH is in development: an iPhone room check for women arriving at hotels, rentals and hostels.

Here is what we are building first:

  • A live-camera room check that turns cold corners warm as they are checked
  • Wi-Fi device naming, with ordinary devices called ordinary
  • A short hidden-lens check from the bed and the shower
  • A report that is shared only through the iOS share sheet
